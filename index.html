Full-Stack · Geospatial Research

MineLand Nexus

Local single-user land research application

  • React 18
  • Python / FastAPI
  • MapLibre
  • SQLite
  • Sentinel-2
  • Local photo AI

Land intelligence for mainland South Africa, bringing public satellite data, environmental history and understandable recommendations into one local workspace. Users locate land through a photo, place search, coordinates or GeoJSON, confirm the boundary, and generate an evidence-linked assessment. Solar and restoration comparisons are preliminary research guidance, with uncertainties and missing evidence made visible.

  • Interactive boundary selection and background assessment jobs
  • Satellite observations, land-cover history, climate and terrain indicators
  • Optional on-device photo descriptions with SmolVLM and llama.cpp
  • Source links, retrieval timestamps and explicit data gaps
  • Printable reports and JSON evidence exports
  • Local SQLite storage and cached public datasets
